# Hairport & Flowers — independent website concept

An independent six-page PRDXQ redesign concept for Hairport & Flowers in Gateway Gardens, Frankfurt. This work was not commissioned, reviewed or approved by the business. It must not be represented as the official website.

## Pages

- index.html — split salon and floristry overview
- damen.html — women's service overview and public price list
- herren.html — men's service overview, public price list and family note
- blumen.html — bouquets, plants and event-decoration services
- kontakt.html — address, opening times, phone, email and route link
- konzept.html — sources, verified facts, limitations and launch notes

## Factual basis

Checked on 16 October 2026. The business's own website describes the salon for women, men and children; personal consultation; styling, colour and care services; a flower boutique with individual bouquets, plants and event decoration; public men's and women's price lists; the address at Amelia-Mary-Earhart-Straße 17, 60549 Frankfurt; telephone 069 71914440; email info@hairportfra.de; and opening hours Monday 10:00–17:00, Tuesday–Friday 10:00–19:00.

The source labels prices as current, but this concept attributes them to the public page and asks visitors to confirm them. No Saturday or Sunday hours were listed on the checked pages. The concept does not invent an online calendar, order system, delivery service, available flower stock, or prices for children or floristry.

The existing Hairport wordmark, logo, photography and reviews were not copied. The illustration and mark here are original design-study assets. The static page has no embedded map, analytics, tracking script, external image, external font, form, or data collection. Route and contact links only open when a visitor clicks them.

## Legal and operational caveats

The concept is marked noindex and discloses that it is independent. Public Pages hosting makes it accessible. Before an official launch, the business would need to approve the name, copy, price list, images and service details. PRDXQ's provider information is not supplied in this concept; complete publisher details and a legal/privacy review are needed before any production use. The public source site's imprint text is noted in the concept page only as a visible source detail, not as a legal assessment.
